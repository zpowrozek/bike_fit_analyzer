## Notes

- Angles are measured at bottom-dead-center (knee most extended) on every stroke and median-ed, so one blurry frame can't skew them. Only seated pedaling counts.
- Amber = within 2.5 deg of the green zone; a phone video resolves angles to a few degrees.
- Saddle fore-aft (setback) isn't measured here. Change one thing at a time, then re-film.
- Knee range is dynamic (30-40 deg): Holmes' static 25-35 deg plus the ~8 deg that video-while-pedaling adds (Bini et al. 2014).
